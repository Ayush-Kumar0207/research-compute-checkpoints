Research Compute Checkpoints

Privacy notice

Effective 1 November 2026. This utility is for the account owner's personal research, rather than a public account service.

Access and purpose

The utility requests Google's https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. It creates, reads, updates and removes its own checkpoint files in a dedicated folder, or files explicitly made available to it. Drive account and storage-quota information is used to confirm the connection and show available backup capacity. It does not request Gmail, contacts or general Drive access.

Storage and processing

The owner-private controller stores the Google OAuth client configuration and offline grant encrypted at rest. Authorized private Kaggle workers use the connection to save and restore research state. Checkpoints, models, run metadata and logs are stored in the dedicated Drive folder; controller state and recovery evidence are also stored in the controller's cloud storage.

Google provides the Drive and authorization services, Kaggle runs the selected jobs, and the dashboard hosting infrastructure processes controller requests and storage. Access is used to operate the owner's selected research jobs and backups. Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or disclosed for unrelated purposes. It is not sent to an external AI provider to train a general-purpose model.

Retention and control

Cleanup protects the latest three verified full snapshots and all files they reference, including retained best and final models and results. Only recognized, unreferenced backup objects older than the configured grace period are eligible for cleanup; invalid backup verification prevents cleanup. Existing historical research folders are excluded.

The owner can pause automatic work using the private dashboard, revoke the app's connection in Google Account permissions, and remove its backup folder in Drive. Revoking access prevents future Drive operations; it does not automatically delete existing research files or controller recovery evidence.

Contact

The app owner's support contact is shown on Google's authorization screen. This information site does not collect messages or offer public sign-up.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
