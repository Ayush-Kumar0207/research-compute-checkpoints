Personal research utility

Research Compute Checkpoints

This personal utility coordinates reproducible research jobs on Kaggle and saves resumable checkpoints to a dedicated Google Drive folder. Its private control room provides start, safe stop, progress and recovery controls for the account owner.

Google Drive access uses only drive.file: the app can work with files it creates or files explicitly made available to it. It does not request general access to the owner's Drive.

Backups are checked before they become the active recovery point. Retention protects the latest three verified snapshots and every model or result those snapshots reference. Historical research folders are outside its cleanup scope.

This page describes the utility. It provides no access to its private dashboard, account connections, research data or checkpoints.
